How to read this plan

Work in ascending step order. Maps look straight down: x increases right; z increases down. Coordinates locate each brick’s minimum x/z grid boundary (top-left on these maps), not its center. The y coordinate is the bottom in plate units (1 plate = 3.2 mm nominal; 1 brick = 3 plates). Rot 90 turns the part a quarter-turn on the base. Colored, numbered rectangles are new pieces; gray rectangles are previous pieces viewed from above. IDs/numbers match the tables. Start base pieces on a flat surface; generated designs include a second backing layer intended to tie them together; check the connected-group diagnostic. Custom plans may not include backing.
